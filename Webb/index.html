About Arcade Haven

Arcade Haven is a modern web-based gaming platform that brings classic arcade games to your browser. Our mission is to provide a nostalgic gaming experience with a modern twist.

All games are built using vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript without any external libraries or frameworks, making them lightweight and fast.

We're constantly working on adding new games and features to enhance your gaming experience. Stay tuned for updates!
